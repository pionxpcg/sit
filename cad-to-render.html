From plan and CAD logic to a believable architectural scene.

Turn architectural plans, CAD-like inputs and schematic design sources into photoreal visualizations while preserving spatial relationships and design intent.

Geometry first

Walls, openings, circulation, fixed elements and visible spatial anchors are treated as authoritative design information.

No silent redesign

Style references guide materials and mood without silently overriding the project geometry.

Geometry is the source of truth

Plan-based rendering should begin with the project geometry, not with a style image. PIONX treats wall positions, openings, circulation, fixed furniture and other visible spatial anchors as authoritative information that should remain stable through the visualization process.

Keep geometry references separate from style references

A plan or CAD-derived image should control layout and spatial relationships, while separate reference images can guide materials, lighting or visual mood. This separation reduces the risk of a reference image silently redesigning the project.

Move from plan logic to a presentation-ready scene

The workflow is designed to convert a plan-led input into a believable architectural interior or exterior view while preserving the relationships that define the source. After the first approved image, Visual DNA can help produce additional consistent views.
