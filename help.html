Help, without the guesswork.

Help for PIONX AI rendering modes, references, visual consistency, CAD-to-render, credits, downloads and account workflows.

Which render mode should I use?

Use Preserve when the existing design and camera must stay closest to the source. Balanced pushes realism while keeping the design. Creative allows more visual exploration.

What should a reference image control?

Use references for style, materials, lighting or project identity. A style image should not silently override authoritative geometry.

Why can a render be rejected?

Production quality checks can reject major geometry drift, wrong scene identity, incorrect aspect ratio, severe artifacts or other hard failures.

What happens after refresh during a render?

The workspace is designed to recover an active server-side render job rather than starting and charging for a new one.
